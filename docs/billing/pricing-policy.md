<h2 id="2-cursor-fees-and-rates" class="anchor-heading group "><a href="#2-cursor-fees-and-rates" class="anchor-link" aria-label="Link to this section"><span class="anchor-icon" aria-hidden="true">#</span></a>2. Cursor Fees and Rates</h2>
<p>Fees for Cursor&#x27;s Services may include subscription fees, usage fees, and other fees applicable to the particular Service. This section sets out how we measure, calculate, or otherwise treat the various fees.</p>
<p><strong>2.1. Precommitted Usage</strong> is the minimum amount of usage fees specified in your Order Form that you commit to pay during your Service Term. Precommitted Usage that is not used within the Service Term will not roll over to any subsequent term.</p>
<p><strong>2.2. On-Demand Usage</strong> applies for usage that: (i) exceeds the Precommitted Usage, and is calculated at the rate specified in your Order Form; or (ii) occurs when you do not have a current Order Form.</p>
<p><strong>2.3. Core License Plans</strong> For Enterprise plans, Precommitted Usage is pooled across users under the same account number.</p>
<p><strong>2.4. Model API Fees</strong> are calculated by multiplying the Total Tokens by the applicable Model Pricing rates. Model Pricing for each model is available at <a href="/docs/models-and-pricing">Models &amp; Pricing</a> and is subject to change in accordance with the Model provider&#x27;s list pricing.</p>
<p><strong>2.5. Cursor Token Fees</strong> are calculated by multiplying the Total Tokens by the applicable Cursor Token Rate.</p>
<p><strong>2.6. Eligible Services</strong> are the following Service elements, the fees for which are eligible to be counted towards your Precommitted Usage:</p>
<ul>
<li>Model API Fees</li>
<li>Cursor Token Fees</li>
<li>Bugbot Usage Fees; Bugbot dynamically selects the optimal model for each task.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>2.7.</strong> Services usage for which you are not charged are not eligible to be counted towards your Precommitted Usage.</p>

<p>Certain pricing models may involve calculations over a period of time, whether monthly, quarterly or other measurement period. Anysphere may offer opportunities to adjust fees based on usage or other measurements, as agreed in your Order Form; the methodology used to calculate such adjustments is described below or in your Order Form.</p>
<p><strong>4.1. The increased total Core License seat count (as at the end of the True Up Period) will be used as the revised True Up Baseline for the next True Up.</p>
